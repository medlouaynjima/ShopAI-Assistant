# Returns and refunds

Customers may initiate a return within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Return shipping is free for defective or wrong items; the buyer pays shipping for other reasons. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after the returned item is received.

Clearance or outlet items and hygiene products such as earbud tips cannot be returned.

A return must be confirmed by the customer and approved by a human. This demo application records approval but does not issue a refund.
